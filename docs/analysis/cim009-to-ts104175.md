# CIM 009 V1.9.1 operation clauses in TS 104 175 V1.1.1

Sources: `docs/spec/` (GS CIM 009 V1.9.1, one file per clause, this repository) and `/workspace/etsi-specs/ts-104175-ngsi-ld-core-api/` (TS 104 175 V1.1.1, 2026-09). The code, `policy::FILTERABLE` and `policy::WHOLE_TENANT` name CIM 009 clauses; the analyses of the `antares-surfaces` epic cite TS 104 175 and use this table to translate.

The full ledger mapping, every clause scored by heading and text, is T-3435's `dev/clause-map.py` and its appendix in `docs/analysis/ts104175-delta.md`. This page is the operations subset the surface analyses cite, with the EntityMap rows that a title match cannot pair filled in by hand.

## 1. Method

Each CIM 009 clause under 5.6 to 5.16 is matched to the TS 104 175 clause of the same title (case and punctuation ignored). The five EntityMap clauses differ only in spelling (`EntityMap` against `Entity Map`) and are matched by hand, and the two `Notification behaviour` clauses are told apart by their chapter (subscriptions against registration subscriptions). Two operations are new in TS 104 175 and have no CIM 009 V1.9.1 clause: 10.2.6 Set Attribute Value and 10.4.4 Retrieve Attribute Value.
